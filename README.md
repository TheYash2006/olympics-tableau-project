# Olympic Games Data Analysis 

## Project Overview

This Tableau project explores Olympic Games data through an interactive
dashboard. It summarizes athlete participation, physical
characteristics, medal performance by country, and medal totals across
sports, years, and seasons.

The dashboard is designed to help users identify long-term trends and
compare countries, sports, and athlete demographics at a glance.

## Dashboard Contents

### 1. Male vs. Female Participation Over Years

A line chart compares the number of female and male participants across
Olympic years.

**Purpose:** Understand how participation has changed over time and
explore the growth of women's participation in the Olympic Games.

### 2. Weight and Height Correlation

A scatter plot compares athletes' height and weight, with each point
representing an athlete record.

**Purpose:** Explore the relationship between height and weight and see
how athlete measurements are distributed.

### 3. Total Athletes by Year and Season

A line chart compares athlete totals for the Summer and Winter Olympic
Games over time.

**Purpose:** Compare participation trends between the two Olympic
seasons and observe changes across editions.

### 4. Top 10 Countries with Total Medals

A horizontal bar chart ranks countries by total medals.

**Purpose:** Identify countries with the highest medal counts and
compare their overall performance.

### 5. Olympic Rings Graphic

The Olympic rings appear in the center of the dashboard as a visual
element reinforcing the Olympic theme.

### 6. Top 10 Sports by Total Medals

A horizontal bar chart ranks sports by total medals.

**Purpose:** See which sports account for the largest medal totals in
the dataset.

### 7. Age Distribution --- Winners

A stacked histogram shows the age distribution of medal-winning
athletes, split by medal type.

**Purpose:** Understand the ages at which medal-winning athletes most
commonly compete and compare age patterns across medal types.

### 8. Weight Distribution --- Winners

A stacked histogram displays the weight distribution of medal-winning
athletes by medal type.

**Purpose:** Explore the distribution of athlete weights among medal
winners.

### 9. Height Distribution --- Winners

A stacked histogram displays the height distribution of medal-winning
athletes by medal type.

**Purpose:** Explore the distribution of athlete heights among medal
winners.

## Key Questions This Dashboard Can Help Answer

-   How has Olympic participation changed over time?
-   How has female participation evolved compared with male
    participation?
-   How do Summer and Winter Olympic athlete totals compare?
-   Which countries have the highest total medal counts?
-   Which sports have the largest medal totals?
-   What age, height, and weight ranges are common among medal-winning
    athletes?
-   How are height and weight related in the athlete data?

## Tools and Techniques

-   **Tableau** --- data visualization and dashboard design
-   **Line charts** --- participation trends over time

-   **Stacked histograms** --- distributions segmented by medal type
-   **Dashboard layout** --- combines multiple views into one overview

## How to Use the Dashboard

1.  Open the Tableau workbook or published dashboard.
2.  Review the charts to compare participation, medals, and athlete
    characteristics.
3.  Hover over marks or bars to inspect the details available in Tableau
    tooltips.
4.  If filters or interactive controls are configured in the workbook,
    use them to narrow the analysis by year, season, country, sport, or
    medal type.
5.  Compare multiple charts to look for patterns. For example, compare
    medal totals by sport with the age or physical-characteristic
    distributions of winners.

*Note: Interactions and filters depend on how they were configured in
the original Tableau workbook.*

## Data Notes and Limitations

-   The dashboard appears to use Olympic athlete and medal records,
    including year, season, country/team, sport, medal, age, height, and
    weight.
-   The screenshot shows **3 null values** in the age, weight, and
    height distribution views. Missing measurements or ages can affect
    the distributions; check the workbook's data-cleaning steps before
    drawing conclusions.
-   The charts show associations and descriptive patterns, not proof of
    cause and effect.
-   Medal totals can depend on how team events, shared medals,
    country/team codes, and historical country names are represented in
    the source data.
-   The screenshot alone does not confirm the exact dataset, date range,
    filters, calculated fields, or data-cleaning rules. Update this
    README if those details are known.

## Suggested Future Improvements

-   Add filters for year, season, country, sport, and medal type.
-   Include KPI cards for total athletes, countries, sports, and medals.
-   Add a clear data-source citation and document any data-cleaning
    rules.
-   Display the exact date range and explain how team-event medals are
    counted.
-   Improve accessibility with descriptive tooltips, readable labels,
    and a color palette that remains distinguishable for users with
    color-vision deficiencies.

## Conclusion

This Tableau dashboard provides a visual overview of Olympic
participation, medal performance, and the physical characteristics of
medal-winning athletes. It brings together trend, comparison, and
distribution charts to support exploration of Olympic history and
athlete data.
